一、适用范围

本隐私政策适用于移动应用「锄大地背唐诗」（英文名 Poker With Tang Poetry，以下简称「本游戏」），说明我们在您使用本游戏时如何处理与您相关的信息。

本游戏的内容（游戏画面、诗词文本、音效等）以本地资源形式打包在应用内，运行于设备本地的网页视图（WebView）中，不需要连接互联网即可完整游玩。

二、我们不收集的信息

本游戏是单机离线游戏，没有注册或登录流程，因此我们不会收集、传输或存储以下任何信息：

  • 姓名、手机号码、电子邮箱、住址等身份信息；
  • 位置信息（GPS 或网络定位）；
  • 通讯录、短信、通话记录、日历；
  • 照片、视频、麦克风录音、摄像头画面；
  • 设备唯一标识符（IMEI、Android ID、广告 ID 等）；
  • 账号凭据、支付信息；
  • 游戏内的操作行为日志、崩溃日志或使用统计。

说明：本游戏未声明网络访问权限，应用本身无法主动与任何服务器通信。

三、仅保存在您设备本地的数据

为了记住您的偏好与进度，本游戏会在设备本地的应用私有存储中保存少量数据（网页本地存储 localStorage / 应用私有目录）。这些数据始终留在您的设备内，不会上传到任何服务器，我们无法访问。

四、设备权限

本游戏在应用清单中未声明、也不申请任何系统权限，包括但不限于：网络、存储、相机、麦克风、位置、通讯录、电话、短信、通知。

因此，本游戏既不会读取您设备上的文件，也无法访问您设备外的任何内容。

五、第三方服务与 SDK

本游戏不集成任何第三方服务、广告平台、统计分析工具或崩溃上报 SDK，也没有社交分享、账号登录、云存档等功能。

关于应用分发平台：当您通过 Google Play 等应用商店下载、更新本游戏时，该商店平台可能会依据其自身的隐私政策收集安装统计、崩溃报告、评价等信息。这些行为由平台独立实施，不属于本游戏的行为，也不受本政策约束，建议您查阅相应平台的政策。

六、信息的共享、转让与公开披露

由于我们不收集、也不持有您的个人信息，因此：

  • 我们不会向任何第三方共享、出售、出租或交易您的信息；
  • 我们不会因商业目的转让您的信息；
  • 我们不会公开披露您的信息；
  • 不存在跨境传输个人信息的情形。

七、数据的保存与删除

7.1 保存位置与期限

所有游戏数据保存在您设备的应用私有存储中，保存期限为「直到您主动删除」。我们不设远程备份，也没有云端副本。

7.2 如何删除

您可以随时通过以下任一方式彻底删除本游戏保存的全部数据：

  • 在系统设置中清除本应用的「存储/数据」（清除数据）；
  • 卸载本游戏（卸载会一并删除应用私有目录内的全部数据）；
  • 在游戏内修改玩家名称、设置等，直接覆盖原有内容。

删除后不可恢复，且无需向我们提出申请——因为数据从未离开您的设备，我们技术上也无法代为删除。

八、数据安全

本游戏运行在系统分配给应用的沙箱内，其他应用无法读取本游戏的私有数据；由于不联网，数据也不存在传输环节被截获的风险。

尽管如此，任何本地存储都无法保证绝对安全（例如设备被解锁、被 root 或系统被入侵）。请您妥善保管设备与锁屏密码。若您更换或出售设备，建议先卸载本游戏或清除应用数据。

九、未成年人保护

本游戏适合所有年龄段用户，内容为中国传统扑克牌玩法与古诗词，不含暴力、恐怖、成人或付费抽奖内容。

由于本游戏不收集任何个人信息，因此不会有意收集 13 周岁以下儿童（或您所在司法辖区规定的相应年龄）的个人信息。若您是家长或监护人，认为孩子在游戏中填写了敏感个人信息（如真实姓名），可在设备上清除应用数据或卸载游戏，并可随时联系我们协助说明。

十、您的权利

依据适用的个人信息保护法规（如《中华人民共和国个人信息保护法》、GDPR 等），您享有知情、决定、查阅、复制、更正、删除其个人信息的权利。就本游戏而言：

  • 查阅/更正：游戏内「修改名称」与「设置」可直接查看和更改本地保存的内容；

我们不会因您行使上述权利而对您进行任何形式的歧视或限制游戏功能。

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application “Poker With Tang Poetry” (Chinese name 「锄大地背唐诗」, hereinafter “the Game”) and explains how information related to you is handled when you use the Game.

All content of the Game — game screens, poetry texts, and audio — is packaged as local assets and runs in a device-local WebView. The Game is fully playable without any internet connection.

2. What We Do Not Collect

The Game is a standalone offline game with no registration or sign-in, so we do not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Identity information such as name, phone number, email address, or postal address;
  • Location data (GPS or network-based);
  • Contacts, SMS, call logs, or calendar;
  • Photos, videos, microphone recordings, or camera images;
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, advertising ID, etc.);
  • Account credentials or payment information;
  • In-game behavioral logs, crash logs, or usage statistics.

Note: the Game declares no internet permission, so the app itself cannot communicate with any server.

3. Local-Only Data

To remember your preferences and progress, the Game stores a small amount of data in the app’s private storage on your device (browser localStorage / app-private directory). This data never leaves your device, is never uploaded to any server, and is inaccessible to us.

Category Content Purpose Uploaded
Player names Nicknames you type for the four seats (defaults: North, East, South, West) Displayed on the table only No
Settings Game speed, table color, AI difficulty, card number/suit size, card-back color, sound and music toggles, volume Restore your preferences No
Scores & coins Round/total scores, coin balance, achievement progress In-game display and settlement No
Play time Daily accumulated play time and a date marker In-game self-management hint No
Language Simplified/Traditional Chinese and Chinese/English display choice Remember interface language No

None of the above contains information that directly identifies you. Player names are entered by you — please do not enter your real name, phone number, or other sensitive details.

4. Device Permissions

The Game declares and requests no system permissions, including but not limited to internet, storage, camera, microphone, location, contacts, phone, SMS, or notifications.

Consequently, the Game cannot read files on your device or access anything outside of it.

5. Third-Party Services and SDKs

The Game integrates no third-party services, advertising networks, analytics tools, or crash-reporting SDKs, and offers no social sharing, account login, or cloud saves.

App stores: when you download or update the Game through Google Play or another store, that platform may collect install statistics, crash reports, and reviews under its own privacy policy. Such processing is performed independently by the platform, is outside the Game’s behavior, and is not governed by this Policy.

6. Sharing, Transfer, and Disclosure

Because we do not collect or hold your personal information:

  • We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your information with any third party;
  • We do not transfer your information for commercial purposes;
  • We do not publicly disclose your information;
  • No cross-border transfer of personal information occurs.

7. Retention and Deletion

7.1 Where and for how long

All game data is stored in the app’s private storage on your device and is retained “until you delete it”. We keep no remote backup and no cloud copy.

7.2 How to delete

You may permanently delete all data saved by the Game at any time by:

  • clearing the app’s storage/data in system settings;
  • uninstalling the Game (this removes all data in the app-private directory);
  • overwriting it in-game (e.g., editing player names or settings).

Deletion is irreversible and no request to us is required — the data never left your device, and we technically cannot delete it on your behalf.

8. Security

The Game runs inside the app sandbox assigned by the operating system, so other apps cannot read its private data; because it is offline, data is not exposed to interception during transmission.

Nevertheless, no local storage is absolutely secure (e.g., if the device is unlocked, rooted, or compromised). Please keep your device and screen lock safe. Before giving away or selling a device, uninstall the Game or clear its data.

9. Children’s Privacy

The Game is suitable for users of all ages. Its content is a traditional Chinese card game plus classical poetry, with no violence, horror, adult content, or paid loot boxes.

Because the Game collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child entered sensitive personal information (such as a real name), you can clear app data or uninstall the Game, and contact us for guidance at any time.

10. Your Rights

Under applicable data-protection laws (e.g., the GDPR and China’s Personal Information Protection Law), you have rights to be informed, to decide, to access, to copy, to rectify, and to delete your personal information. For the Game:

  • Access / rectification: use “Edit Names” and “Settings” in the Game to view and change locally stored content;
  • Deletion: clear app data or uninstall the Game to remove all local data;
  • Withdrawing consent: the Game performs no consent-based collection; toggles such as sound and music take effect immediately;
  • Complaints: you may lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority, or contact us first to resolve the matter.

We will not discriminate against you or restrict game features in any way for exercising these rights.

11. Changes to This Policy

If the Game’s functionality changes (for example, if internet access, accounts, or analytics are added in the future), we will update this Policy on this page and mark the new “Last updated” date at the top.

For material changes affecting your rights, we will highlight them in the app or describe them in the store release notes. Please review this page periodically.
